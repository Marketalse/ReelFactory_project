## 1. Floating software interface
Create a clean, modern floating software interface animation. Display a realistic browser window with a simplified AI application dashboard. The interface appears inside a rounded white card with subtle shadows. Animate the card sliding upward from the bottom of the screen, followed by a gentle scale-in effect. Highlight the important interface element using a warm orange accent. Maintain a premium educational video aesthetic. Keep the background transparent or clean, with no unnecessary decorations.

## 2. Floating document card (prompt structure)
Create an elegant floating document card showing an AI prompt structure. Use a white paper-like interface, rounded corners, subtle drop shadow and clean typography. Animate the document entering from the right, expanding slightly, then reveal highlighted text lines sequentially. Use orange and yellow highlighting for important phrases. Keep all text editable and avoid generating random unreadable text.

## 3. Presenter + lower-third information card (9:16)
Design a vertical 9:16 educational video composition. Keep the real presenter centered in the upper-middle area. Introduce a floating rounded information card in the lower third, containing a software screenshot, one large headline and a small explanatory caption. Use subtle shadows, soft depth, smooth slide-in animation and restrained motion. Maintain clear separation between the presenter and the information layer.

## 4. Pixel-art marketing department
Create a high-quality 16-bit pixel-art illustration of a large marketing department. Show dozens of small office-worker characters arranged in organized rows. Use a dark navy background, detailed pixel characters, subtle movement and a limited yellow, white and blue color palette. Animate the characters appearing progressively in groups, creating the impression of a growing marketing team. Maintain consistent pixel dimensions and character proportions.

## 5. Pixel-art social media manager character
Design a consistent 16-bit pixel-art business character representing a social media manager. The character wears modern office clothing, holds a laptop and has a friendly professional appearance. Use a transparent background. Animate a short idle movement, gentle bounce and a small hand gesture. Keep the character's proportions and pixel-art style consistent with the rest of the scene.

## 6. Pixel-art meeting scene (top-down)
Create a top-down 16-bit pixel-art office meeting scene with a large round conference table and several business characters seated around it. Add laptops, documents and small presentation screens. Use a deep navy background with yellow and blue details. Animate the characters moving slightly, with documents and information icons appearing around the table. Maintain a clean composition suitable for a vertical educational video.
