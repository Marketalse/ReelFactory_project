# AI Tool Reel Teardown

Source: `Downloads/AI Tool Reel Teardown.md`, captured 2026-10-31.

Five reels, same creator. 9:16 · 720×1280 · 30 fps · 32–42 s · 8–10 layout cuts · visual change ≈ every 1 s.

## Formula
- Hook 0–3 s: title + claim
- Reveal 3–6 s: "It's called…" / problem
- Punch: 1 s face
- Proof body 6–32 s: 3–5 beats, "it does X → screen shows X", stat pops, "the best part, it's free"
- CTA last 3–5 s: comment "WORD"

## Layout modes
A. Split: screen card top 35%, captions middle, face rounded card bottom 40%
B. Full B-roll: face hidden, larger screen card on dark vignette, slow push-in
C. White card: pure white chapter card, tool name under logo/screenshot
D. Face punch-in: full-frame close-up ~1 s on key word
E. CTA: full-frame face, italic "comment" above big yellow glowing keyword in quotes

## Edit rules
- 1–3 words on screen, synced word by word
- Bold geometric sans captions, centered
- One keyword per phrase swaps to italic serif in accent color
- White on dark, near-black on light
- Hook title: line 1 plain bold sans, line 2 italic serif on solid accent box, types word by word in first second
- Screen cards: rebuilt/cleaned UI, thin glowing border, soft shadow, animates every ~1 s
- Stat pills: dark rounded box, huge white number, label underneath
- Transitions: quick blur or whip, not hard cuts
- Talking head: 4K wide, grey wall, mic in frame, eyes on lens; every breath/pause cut out, no silence >0.25 s
- Audio: low music bed, whooshes on mode changes, pops on stat callouts

## Script pattern
| Hook title | Reveal | Proof beats | Stat / free hook | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [tool] claim | It's called X / problem | 3–5 beats, each = one sentence + screen | standout stat / free/open source | comment "WORD" |

## Production steps
1. Pick tool + keyword + CTA word
2. Write script with prompt 1
3. Capture proof screens separately
4. Shoot talking head in 4K, one take
5. Cut and caption word by word
6. Assemble modes A→B→C→D→E per timeline, blur transitions, stat pills, music + SFX
7. Export H.264 1080×1920 30 fps
