How it works

Your footage is one track. Click a piece to select it, drag its left or right edge to trim, Split at the playhead to cut it, Delete to remove a piece, ◀ ▶ to reorder.

Check my cuts proposes cuts (long pauses, silences, repeated words). Accept or Reject each one, or accept all.

Auto-edit builds new footage from this timeline, then the editor picks the story, cuts the pauses, adds captions, cards and zooms and makes a draft. It costs about the same as a new reel. Your earlier version is kept: Undo brings it back.
